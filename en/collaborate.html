COLLABORATE

Clear work. Clear ownership. Clear upside.

If your work materially helps a project create value, compensation should be explicit before serious work begins.

01

Fixed pay

Best for clearly scoped work with a defined deliverable.

02

Project revenue share

For meaningful contributions that materially affect a project's long-term outcome.

03

Hybrid

A smaller fixed fee plus defined upside when both sides want shared risk and reward.

BEFORE WORK STARTS

Six things should be written down.

01Scope

What is being done, by whom and by when.

02Ownership

Who owns the work, code, research and assets.

03Revenue definition

Exactly which revenue is included in the agreement.

04Compensation

Fixed pay, share, cap, duration or hybrid.

05Exit

What happens if the project or collaboration stops.

06Reporting

How project revenue and costs are reported.

SIMPLE EXAMPLE

Share what is actually distributable.

Gross project revenue$1,000
Payment, refund & direct costs−$120
Approved recoupment−$80
Distributable project revenue$800

The real split is defined per project. This example is not a promise of returns, equity or employment.

GOOD FIT

Research. Engineering. Product. Distribution. Domain expertise.

Bring work that can materially improve a project. Tiny tasks should not create permanent economic rights.
