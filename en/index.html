03 / How I work

Curiosity is the start. Evidence is the edit.

I like ideas, but I trust the version that survives a test. I try to make the work legible: what I checked, what I found, what I couldn’t establish, and what I’d do next.

01

Start with a specific question

Define the real problem before choosing a tool or a format.

02

Make the test repeatable

Keep examples, sources and limits visible so someone else can check the work.

03

Leave room to be wrong

Separate observations from assumptions. Update the answer when the evidence changes.
