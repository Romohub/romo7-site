01 / ROMO7 METHOD

From a real question to sustainable value.

Every Romo7 project starts with the problem, earns its way through evidence, and only grows when the value and economics make sense.

01 Ask

Start with a useful question, not a technology looking for a reason to exist.

02 Test

Try the idea quickly and at the smallest useful scale.

03 Build

Turn the evidence into something simple people can actually use.

04 Measure

Check usefulness, quality and cost with real signals.

05 Earn

Grow only what creates real value and has workable economics.
