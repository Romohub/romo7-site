03THE ECONOMY

Revenue is designed in, not added later.

Before a project gets serious time, we define who it serves, how it can earn, what it costs, and how contributors participate.

01

Products

Subscriptions, premium features, paid tools and one-time purchases.

02

Research & intelligence

Reports, data, testing, evaluation and high-value research work.

03

Partnerships

Selective sponsored work, integrations and distribution partnerships that fit the product.

04

Shared ventures

Project-specific revenue share for builders whose contribution materially affects the outcome.

1Gross project revenuesales / subscriptions / partnerships
→
2Direct costs & recoupmentfees / APIs / infrastructure / approved advances
→
3Project pooldocumented split for the people who built it
→
4Reinvestgrowth / product / future experiments

Revenue share is project-specific, documented before work begins, and is not a promise of guaranteed income. Equity, investment or employment terms only exist when separately agreed in writing.

04WHY THIS MODEL
01

No fake “exposure” jobs

If meaningful work creates value, compensation should be explicit.

02

No permanent split for tiny tasks

Shares can be permanent, time-limited, capped, milestone-based or mixed with fixed pay.

03

Transparent project accounting

Revenue, direct costs, approved recoupment and split logic should be understandable by contributors.
