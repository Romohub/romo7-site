ROMO7 / COLLABORATE

No vague promises. Define the work and the upside.

Romo7 can work with contributors, specialists, partners and co-builders. The structure depends on the project, but the economics should be understandable before serious work begins.

01THE RULE
R7 //
If your work materially helps a project create value, your compensation should be explicit.
02COMPENSATION MODES
01

Fixed pay

Best for clearly scoped tasks where the value and effort are known upfront.

EXAMPLE: testing, translation, a defined implementation
02

Revenue share

A documented percentage of defined project revenue for meaningful ongoing contribution.

EXAMPLE: core engineering, distribution, product ownership
03

Capped upside

A share continues until a fixed payout cap is reached. Useful for medium-sized contributions.

EXAMPLE: earn 4% until a defined total payout
04

Time-limited share

Revenue share for a defined period when the contribution matters but should not create permanent dilution.

EXAMPLE: 12 or 24 months after launch
05

Recoup + share

Approved project expenses are paid back from project revenue before the normal split begins.

EXAMPLE: server, API, launch or production costs
06

Hybrid

Fixed pay plus smaller upside when both immediate compensation and long-term alignment make sense.

EXAMPLE: fee + small revenue share
03BEFORE WORK STARTS
01
Scope

What exactly are we building or delivering?

02
Ownership

Who owns code, data, media, accounts and IP?

03
Revenue definition

Which revenue is included, and what direct costs are deducted first?

04
Split

Percentage, cap, duration, milestones and payment timing.

05
Exit

What happens if someone stops contributing or the project pauses?

06
Reporting

How contributors can understand project revenue and payouts.
